Frequently Asked Questions

What is TrustLayer protection?

TrustLayer uses advanced behavioral biometrics to monitor your typing cadence and mouse movement speed. Even if an attacker steals your passwords, they cannot duplicate your movement rhythm, preventing fraud.

How does the 24-hour payee cooldown work?

When you register a new payee, there is a 24-hour security window. During this time, transfers to that payee are limited to ₹5,000 as a safeguard against immediate transfer attempts by intruders.

What should I do if my account is frozen?

If the system detects anomalies, it may freeze the session. Please contact the security operations desk (our demo dashboard unfreeze control) to unfreeze the session.
