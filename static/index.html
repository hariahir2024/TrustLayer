Continuous Protection Life Cycle

01

Silent Enrollment

Users register by typing a short passphrase 5 times. Our light-weight JS SDK captures key timing down to milliseconds. No password or private text is collected or stored.

02

Telemetry Streams

During active sessions, mouse velocities, path shapes, and key frequencies are sampled and pushed in real-time. Perfectly straight mouse lines and 0ms flight times flag bot scripts instantly.

03

Fusion Scoring

A local Z-Score Profiler and Isolation Forest model calculate keystroke and mouse anomaly indices. A composite risk score (0-100) is refreshed dynamically.

04

Adaptive Response

Low risk scores pass seamlessly. Medium scores trigger step-up typing re-authentication. High scores freeze the session and issue fraud alerts to bank administrators.
